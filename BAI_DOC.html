Lớp Học Tiếng Anh 6 - Kỹ Năng Đọc Hiểu

Lý thuyết trọng tâm dạng bài Đọc Hiểu

Dạng 1: Điền từ vào đoạn văn (Gap-filling)

  • Bước 1: Đọc lướt toàn bộ đoạn văn để hiểu ngữ cảnh chung.
  • Bước 2: Nhìn vào chỗ trống và các từ xung quanh để xác định loại từ cần điền (danh từ, động từ, giới từ, tính từ...).
  • Bước 3: Chú ý đến ngữ pháp cơ bản: thì của động từ, danh từ số nhiều/số ít.

Dạng 2: Đọc hiểu chọn đáp án đúng (Multiple Choice)

  • Skimming: Đọc lướt để tìm ý chính của toàn đoạn (trả lời câu "What is the passage mainly about?").
  • Scanning: Đọc quét tìm thông tin chi tiết. Gạch chân từ khóa (keywords) trong câu hỏi để tìm trong đoạn.
  • Đoán nghĩa từ vựng / Đại từ: Đọc câu chứa từ đó hoặc câu ngay trước nó để tìm manh mối.

ĐỀ 1. BÀI TẬP MẪU (CÓ ĐÁP ÁN)

Hướng dẫn: Bấm nút "Xem đáp án" ở từng câu để kiểm tra.

Exercise 1. Mark the correct word to fill in the blank.

Susan is my best friend. She is tall and has long black (1)________. She lives in an apartment. Her apartment has a pool but she rarely (2)________ to the swimming pool. Her favorite subject (3)________ IT because she really likes using computers. She goes to the IT club at 5.30 p.m. every Friday. She is very (4)________. She always helps people when they need help. Susan and I have a festival (5)________ school next week. It starts at 10 a.m. and ends at 9 p.m.

1. A. eye    B. arm    C. nose    D. hair

Đáp án: D

2. A. goes    B. go    C. has    D. have

Đáp án: A

3. A. is    B. am    C. are    D. was

Đáp án: A

4. A. helpful    B. selfish    C. active    D. funny

Đáp án: A

5. A. in    B. at    C. on    D. by

Đáp án: B

Exercise 3. Read the passage and choose the correct answer.

My name is Nam, and I am a student in class 6B at Sunshine Secondary School. I live with my parents and my younger sister in a small house near a park. I usually ride my bike to school because it is about two kilometres from my house. My favourite subject is English, and I have English lessons three times a week. After school, I often play badminton with my classmates in the school playground.

My younger sister is Hoa. She is eleven years old and loves animals. She has a small white cat named Snowy. Every afternoon, Hoa feeds the cat and plays with it in the garden. Today, she is sitting on the sofa and reading a book about wild animals, while I am doing my English homework at the table.

1. What is the passage mainly about?
A. Nam's school subjects and teachers.
B. Nam's family and daily activities.
C. Hoa's favorite books and school.
D. Nam's favorite sports and animals.

Đáp án: B

2. How does Nam usually go to school?
A. By bus.    B. On foot.    C. By bike.    D. By car.

Đáp án: C

3. The word "younger" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to ________.
A. not as old as someone else.
B. taller than someone else.
C. more beautiful than someone else.
D. more intelligent than someone else.

Đáp án: A

4. According to the passage, which of the following is NOT TRUE?
A. Nam lives with his parents and his younger sister.
B. Nam has English lessons three times a week.
C. Hoa has a small black cat named Snowy.
D. Hoa loves animals.

Đáp án: C

5. The word "it" in paragraph 2 refers to ________.
A. the garden.    B. the book.    C. the sofa.    D. the cat.

Đáp án: D
